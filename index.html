Tap dice to build a pool; the number on the left is how many each tap adds. Hold a die for advantage, exploding, and the rest. Any number of sides works, from d1 to d1000, so a d17 or a d57 is just as valid. Rolls stay on your device.

Add this to your home screen and it works offline, with no connection needed.

    Share rolls

    Rolls are encrypted with the passphrase before they leave your device, so the relay only ever sees ciphertext and holds nothing once everyone has gone. This copy was served over the network, so you are also trusting whoever serves it to keep sending honest code — the app updates itself on every load. Download the single file, or self-host, for a guarantee that does not rest on that. Either way each player's device reports its own rolls, so a modified client could report anything.
